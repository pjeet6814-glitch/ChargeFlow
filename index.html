Verified Scientific & Algorithmic Foundation: ChargeFlow schedules charging slots based on slack = (t_departure − t) − (E_remaining / P_max). Feasible requests with tight slack (≤ 1 h) receive priority dispatch; non-urgent jobs are allocated via shortest-job-first; infeasible requests that cannot possibly reach target are transparently deferred rather than starving achievable sessions. Constant charging efficiency and constant power within 1-minute steps are assumed. Cap is guaranteed never exceeded.
